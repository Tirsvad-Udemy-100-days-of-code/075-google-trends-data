# Summary

What Day 75 teaches:

- Reading and exploring data with `describe()`, and what Google Trends numbers mean.
- Finding and removing missing values with `isna()` and `dropna()`.
- Converting strings to datetimes and resampling daily data to monthly data.
- Charts with two y axes, colours, size, resolution, limits and fonts.
- Time axis locators, line styles, markers and grids.
- Rolling averages, and the difference between a leading and a lagging indicator.
- Layered code: pandas and Matplotlib stay in the infrastructure layer, so the rules are easy to test.

**What you gain:** a small, tested project you can run and compare with your own solution.
