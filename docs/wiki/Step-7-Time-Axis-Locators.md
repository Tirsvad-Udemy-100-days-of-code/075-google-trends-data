# Step 7: Time axis locators

Locators put tick marks where a time line needs them. A `YearLocator` marks the years (major ticks), a `MonthLocator` marks the months (minor ticks) and a `DateFormatter("%Y")` writes the year. With the ticks in place the spike of March 2016 (the unveiling of the Tesla Model 3) is easy to date.
