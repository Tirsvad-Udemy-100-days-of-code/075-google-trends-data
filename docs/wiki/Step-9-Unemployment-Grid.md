# Step 9: Unemployment and the grid

Searches for "Unemployment Benefits" in the U.S. against the unemployment rate (FRED). `ax1.grid(color="grey", linestyle="--")` adds a grid. The vertical lines line up with spikes in December, which shows seasonality. The financial crisis of 2008 raised the rate, and it took about ten years to return to the earlier level. The search spike at the end of 2013 came without a rise in the rate.

![Step 9: Unemployment and the grid](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/075-google-trends-data/raw/branch/main/docs/images/unemployment-grid.png)

**What you gain:** A grid helps you see seasonality in a time series and read exact years and values.
