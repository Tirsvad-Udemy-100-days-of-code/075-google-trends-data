# Step 8: Bitcoin

The same chart for Bitcoin news searches against the resampled monthly price. The price is a dashed orange line (`linestyle="--"`, limits 0 to 15000) and the search points are small circles (`marker="o"`). The dates come from the index of the monthly price. The record search volume at the start of 2018 goes with the price peak; the price rise of early 2019 brought a much smaller rise in searches.

![Step 8: Bitcoin](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/075-google-trends-data/raw/branch/main/docs/images/bitcoin.png)

**What you gain:** Line styles and markers, and reusing a chart with a different dataset by taking the dates from the resampled index.
